BONZI'S OPENING REPERTOIRE
==========================

1. THE SCHOLAR'S MATE (1.e4 e5 2.Bc4 Nc6 3.Qh5 Nf6?? 4.Qxf7#)
   My signature. Four moves, one dead king, zero remorse. Every
   serious player has a defense for this. You are not every serious
   player.

2. THE ITALIAN GAME (1.e4 e5 2.Nf3 Nc6 3.Bc4)
   Named for Italy, where they also invented the fork. Coincidence?
   Yes. But an elegant one.

3. THE SICILIAN DEFENSE (1.e4 c5)
   What you play when you have read one book about chess and want
   everyone to know it. Respectable. Sharp. You will still lose.

4. THE QUEEN'S GAMBIT (1.d4 d5 2.c4)
   I offer you a pawn. You take the pawn. I take the center, the
   initiative, your rook, and eventually your afternoon.

5. THE BONGCLOUD (1.e4 e5 2.Ke2)
   Objectively terrible. Spiritually undefeated. I play it when I
   want you to know that the outcome was never in doubt.
